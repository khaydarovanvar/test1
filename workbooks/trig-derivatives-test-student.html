Differentiating sin, cos, tan, ex and ln x

Thirty questions on the trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic derivatives, in three tiers of ten.

Pure Mathematics 1, 7.3–7.4 · Pure Mathematics 2, 4.1–4.4 · 30 questions · 60 marks

KEY WORDS

  • natural logarithm

REMEMBER
  1. (sin x)′ = cos x, (cos x)′ = −sin x, (tan x)′ = sec2x.
  2. (ex)′ = ex and (ln x)′ = 1x.
  3. Chain rule: differentiate the outside, then multiply by the derivative of the inside.
  4. ddxln f(x) = f′(x)f(x).
  5. Radians throughout. Show the rule you are using — method carries marks even when the answer slips.
