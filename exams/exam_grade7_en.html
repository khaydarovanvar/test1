Part 1. READING — Read the text and answer questions 21–25.

The Vegetable Orchestra

In the city of Vienna, there is a very unusual group of musicians called the Vegetable Orchestra. Instead of buying normal instruments, the members make their own instruments from fresh vegetables. They use carrots to make flutes, pumpkins to make drums, and peppers to make trumpets. Before every concert, the musicians go to the market and spend several hours choosing and cutting the vegetables, because each vegetable must be exactly the right shape to produce a good sound.

The orchestra has been playing concerts around the world for more than twenty years. Their music sounds strange but beautiful, and audiences are always surprised by it. After each performance, the musicians do not throw the vegetables away. Instead, the cooks make hot vegetable soup and share it with the audience. In this way, nothing is wasted, and everybody leaves the concert with something to enjoy.
