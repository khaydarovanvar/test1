The Paralympic Games

The Paralympic Games are a major sports event for athletes with physical disabilities. They take place every four years, a few weeks after the Olympic Games, and in the same host city. The first Paralympic Games were held in Rome in 1960, when only 400 athletes from 23 countries took part. Today, thousands of athletes from more than 160 countries compete, and millions of people watch the events on television.

Paralympic athletes train just as hard as Olympic athletes, sometimes even harder. Many of them have inspiring stories: some were born with a disability, while others had accidents that changed their lives. Instead of giving up, they decided to become professional sportspeople. Their achievements show that determination is more important than physical strength alone. Because of this, the Paralympic Games are not only about winning medals — they also change the way people think about disability.
