Anatomy of a lesson

Forty minutes, five moves

Every topic page is laid out in the order you actually teach it, with the clock written in.

01 · 5 min

Warm-up & recall

Three quick questions from the previous lesson, on the board before the register is done.

02 · 12 min

Explanation

The idea, stated plainly, with every definition, rule and figure a learner needs — drawn, not described.

03 · 8 min

Interactive model

A live model on the board: drag a vertex, move a slider, watch the rule hold. One per topic.

04 · 13 min

Practice

Twenty-one problems in three graded sets — 7 easy, 7 medium, 7 hard — with answers you can reveal.

05 · 2 min

Homework

Four to six problems and a textbook reference. Short enough to be done, hard enough to matter.
