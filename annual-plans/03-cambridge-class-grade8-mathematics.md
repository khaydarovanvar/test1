# Annual Plan — Mathematics, Grade 8 (Cambridge Class)

**6 hours per week · 204 hours per year · Algebra and Geometry taught together · Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics Stage 9, strengthened with the Uzbekistan Grade 8 programme**

Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics Stage 9 on its own is lighter than the Uzbekistan Grade 8
programme: it contains no quadratic equations, no trigonometry, no circle theorems, no vectors,
no coordinate geometry and no work with algebraic fractions. Taught alone over 6 hours a week it
would leave this class **behind** the national special class.

This plan therefore uses **Stage 9 as the backbone and the national Grade 8 programme as the depth
layer**. All 15 Stage 9 units and all 6 Stage 9 projects are covered, and the following national
topics are built in as full teaching blocks (marked *UZ extension*):

* arithmetic roots of degree n and powers with rational exponents
* algebraic fractions and fractional-rational expressions
* absolute and relative error
* systems of inequalities, modulus equations and inequalities
* **quadratic equations** — factorising, completing the square, the discriminant, Vieta's theorem,
  biquadratic and reducible equations, problem solving (18 hours)
* parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, square, trapezium; Thales' theorem; midlines
* **right-triangle trigonometry** — sin, cos, tan, cot; the basic identity; solving triangles (10 hours)
* areas of polygons, the coordinate method, the equation of a circle and a line, **vectors**
* **circle theorems** — tangents, inscribed angles, secants, chords
* elements of combinatorics and the basic counting principle

The quarter hour totals (54 / 42 / 60 / 48) are the standard Uzbek quarter lengths
(9 / 7 / 10 / 8 weeks) multiplied by 6 hours, so this class runs on the same school calendar,
the same quarters and the same holidays as the national classes.

Assessment: 8 topic tests plus a quarter assessment at the end of each quarter, in Cambridge
Checkpoint format (Paper 1 non-calculator, Paper 2 calculator).

## Public holidays ("red days") and how they are absorbed

Uzbekistan public holidays that fall on a working school day remove real lessons from the plan.
The plan below keeps the **official hour totals unchanged** and absorbs the loss through the
lessons marked `R` (reserve) in the Flag column.

| Date | Holiday | Weekday (2026–2027) | Effect on the plan |
|---|---|:--:|---|
| 1 September | Independence Day | Tue | Falls before the first school day (2 September) — no lesson lost. |
| 1 October | Day of Teachers and Mentors | Thu | 1 school day lost — Quarter I. |
| 8 December | Constitution Day | Tue | 1 school day lost — Quarter II. |
| 1 January | New Year | Fri | Falls inside the winter holiday — no lesson lost. |
| 14 January | Day of Defenders of the Motherland | Thu | 1 school day lost — Quarter III. |
| 8 March | International Women's Day | Mon | 1 school day lost — Quarter III. |
| 21 March | Navruz | Sun | Normally inside the spring holiday; the day off in lieu may cost 1 school day. |
| Ramazon Hayit (movable) | Eid al-Fitr | — | Approx. late March 2027; usually adjoins the spring holiday. Confirm annually. |
| 9 May | Day of Remembrance and Honour | Sun | Day off in lieu on Monday 10 May — 1 school day lost, Quarter IV. |
| Qurbon Hayit (movable) | Eid al-Adha | — | Approx. late May 2027; may fall after the last lesson. Confirm annually. |

**Expected loss:** 4–6 school days per year fall on a working day. For a subject taught
3 hours a week this typically costs **2–3 lessons**; for 2 hours a week, **1–2 lessons**;
for the 6-hour Cambridge course, **4–6 lessons**.

**Absorption protocol**

1. At the start of each quarter, count the red days that fall on the days when this class has mathematics.
2. Remove exactly that many `R` lessons from the same quarter, working from the **end** of the quarter backwards.
3. Never remove a `Test` lesson and never remove a lesson that introduces new content.
4. If a quarter has fewer `R` lessons than lessons lost, merge two consecutive lessons on the same
   topic (the national plan deliberately repeats topic titles across 2–3 lessons for this purpose).
5. Record every adjustment in the register so that the quarter still shows the official hour total.

**School holidays** (autumn ~1–7 November, winter ~30 December – 10 January, spring ~20–30 March)
are already excluded from the quarter hour totals and need no adjustment.

> The weekday column is calculated for the **2026–2027** academic year. The two movable Islamic
> holidays and any government resolution transferring a working day must be confirmed against the
> Cabinet of Ministers resolution published each year, and the protocol above re-applied.
