02 · THE REARRANGEMENT

Four copies of the same triangle, inside a square of side a + b. Watch where the leftover area goes.

(a + b)²  =  4 · ½ab  +  c²
a² + 2ab + b²  =  2ab + c²
a² + b²  =  c²
left over in the tilted square: c²  ·  left over in the two corners: a² + b²
