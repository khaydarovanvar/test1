Section A · Easy Oson · Лёгкие

One step each — read the numbers straight off the formula.

  1. A1Find the slope of the line through A(1, 2) and B(5, 10).
  2. A2A line has slope 3 and passes through (0, −1). What is y when x = 4?
  3. A3Find the gradient of the line through (−2, 5) and (4, 5).
  4. A4For f(x) = x2, find the slope of the secant joining x = 1 and x = 4.
  5. A5For f(x) = x2, find the slope of the secant joining x = 2 and x = 5.
  6. A6For f(x) = 3x + 1, find the secant slope between x = 1 and x = 6. What do you notice?
  7. A7Given f′(x) = 2x, find the gradient of the tangent to y = x2 at x = 3.
  8. A8Given f′(x) = 2x, where is the tangent to y = x2 horizontal?
  9. A9For f(x) = x2, simplify f(2+h) − f(2)h.
  10. A10A curve has f′(4) = 7. Say in words what this means.

Section B · Medium Oʻrta · Средние

Two or three steps: set it up, simplify, then let h → 0.

  1. B1Find the equation of the line through (2, 3) and (6, 11).
  2. B2For f(x) = x2, find the secant slope between x = 3 and x = 3 + h.
  3. B3Use the definition of the derivative to find f′(x) for f(x) = 5x − 4.
  4. B4Use the definition to find f′(x) for f(x) = x2 + 3x.
  5. B5Find the equation of the tangent to y = x2 at x = 2. Use f′(x) = 2x.
  6. B6For f(x) = x3, find the secant slope between x = 1 and x = 2. Compare it with f′(1) = 3.
  7. B7For f(x) = x2 at x = 2, find the secant slopes for h = 0.1, 0.01, 0.001. What number are they approaching?
  8. B8Use the definition to find f′(x) for f(x) = x2 − 4x + 1.
  9. B9At which point on y = x2 is the gradient equal to 10?
  10. B10A car travels s(t) = t2 metres in t seconds. Find its average speed from t = 2 to t = 5, and its speed at t = 2 given s′(t) = 2t.

Section C · Hard Qiyin · Трудные

Algebra with letters, and questions that ask you to explain.

  1. C1Use first principles to find f′(x) for f(x) = 1x.
  2. C2Use first principles to find f′(x) for f(x) = √x.
  3. C3Use first principles to find f′(x) for f(x) = x3.
  4. C4Find the equation of the tangent to y = x2 − 2x at the point where the gradient is 4. Use y′ = 2x − 2.
  5. C5Show that the tangent to y = x2 at x = a has equation y = 2ax − a2.
  6. C6The tangent to y = x2 at x = a passes through (0, −4). Find a.
  7. C7Find the points on y = x3 − 3x where the tangent is horizontal. Use y′ = 3x2 − 3.
  8. C8For f(x) = x2, show that the secant slope between x = a and x = b is a + b. What happens as b → a?
  9. C9A ball has height h(t) = 20t − 5t2 metres after t seconds. (a) Find its average velocity from t = 1 to t = 3. (b) Use first principles to find h′(t). (c) When is the ball momentarily at rest?
  10. C10For f(x) = |x|: the secant between x = −1 and x = 1 has slope 0. Explain why the graph still has no tangent at x = 0.
