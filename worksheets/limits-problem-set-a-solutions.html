    limx→4 x2 − 16x − 4
    = 8
    = limx→4 (x − 4)(x + 4)x − 4
    M1
    Substitution gives 0/0, so factorise the difference of two squares.
    = limx→4 (x + 4),  x ≠ 4
    A1
    = 4 + 4 = 8
    A1

    = 18
