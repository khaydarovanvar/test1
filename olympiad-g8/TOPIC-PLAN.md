# Grade 8 olympiad mathematics — the topic breakdown

Aimed at three things at once: **AMC 8**, **Math Kangaroo (Cadet, grades 7–8)**
and the **Art of Problem Solving** introductory sequence. Nineteen topics in
five branches, ordered so that each one needs only what came before it.

The breakdown lives in `content/topics.py`; this file is the readable copy.

---

## Why these nineteen

AMC 8 is 25 questions in 40 minutes, so it rewards recognition over machinery:
the same handful of themes come back every year. Kangaroo adds what AMC has
little of — visual, spatial and pure-logic problems. AoPS adds the depth that
turns an AMC 8 competitor into an AMC 10 one. The breakdown is sized to that:
roughly a quarter of each paper is geometry, a quarter is counting and logic,
and ratios/percents appear somewhere in nearly every paper, usually more than
once.

Branch sizes follow that weighting: **Numbers 4**, **Algebra 4**,
**Geometry 6**, **Counting and logic 4**, **Mock papers 1**.

## What one written topic contains

The three written so far follow the same shape as the Grades 7–11 olympiad
lessons, resized for Grade 8:

- **Goals** — five or six lines of what the reader will be able to do.
- **Six to nine theory sections**, built from definitions, theorems **with
  full proofs**, worked examples with numbered steps, techniques, notes,
  warnings about the standard traps, tables and figures.
- **A closing section of the moves** that actually solve problems in that
  topic — ten or eleven lines, the thing to re-read the night before.
- **Forty-six to forty-nine problems** in four levels, each with a hint and a
  full solution, filterable on the page by Easy / Medium / Hard / Olympiad.

Everything is bilingual (Russian and English) on one button.

## Why 1, 2 and 9 were written first

Topics 1 and 2 are the first two of the course and the two that everything
later leans on. Topic 9 was pulled forward out of order deliberately: it is
the figure-heavy one, so between the three you can judge both a computational
topic and a geometric one — the proofs, the worked examples, the problem
levels and the SVG diagrams — before the remaining sixteen are written to the
same pattern.
