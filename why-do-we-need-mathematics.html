Section 09 · Mathematics, money & Islamic finance

Is interest haram?

In Islamic finance, riba (interest / usury) is prohibited, so conventional interest-based lending is generally regarded as impermissible under Islamic principles.

This is not a theological debate, and I am not the one to settle it. What mathematics gives you is separate and still essential: the ability to read any contract, compare structures, and see the consequences before you sign.
